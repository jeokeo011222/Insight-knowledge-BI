- models/semantic_model.go: 三个表全部加 CertifiedAt *time.Time 字段
  * SemanticModel.CertifiedAt
  * SemanticField.CertifiedAt
  * SemanticRelationship.CertifiedAt
  GORM AutoMigrate 自动补列

- semantic/certification.go: 完整认证服务
  * CertifyModel / DecertifyModel (级联所有 field + relationship)

  * CertifyRelationship / DecertifyRelationship (单个 join)

    → 提取 SELECT 聚合表达式 → 生成 draft SemanticField(kind=metric)
  * BatchAdoptFromRecent: cron 友好版, 扫最近 N 天相同表达式出现 ≥ minCount 次
  * 自动 dedupe + 跳过已存在 + 推断类型 + 派生名称

- api/semantic.go: 三个新端点
  * POST /api/v1/semantic/model/{id}/certify {"certified":true|false}
  * POST /api/v1/semantic/field/{id}/certify {"certified":true|false}
  * POST /api/v1/semantic/relationship/{id}/certify {"certified":true|false}
